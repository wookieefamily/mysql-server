Crosspup

One pup per yard

  • One pup per row, per column, and per coloured yard. Every yard gets exactly one — no more, no fewer.
  • Pups won't sit next to each other. Not side by side, not stacked, not even corner to corner.
  • Tap a square once to cross it out — that's your note for "no pup here". Tap again to put a pup down. A third tap clears it.
  • Right-click or long-press drops a pup straight in. Keyboard: ↑↓←→ move, Space cycles, X crosses out, P places, U undo, H hint.
  • A hint is always a step you could have worked out yourself — never a peek at the answer.
