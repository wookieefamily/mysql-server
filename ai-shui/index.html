How to hold this. Feng shui (風水, “wind–water”) is a several-thousand-year-old Chinese practice of arranging space; this app applies the Western/Black-Sect bagua overlay, the wu xing five-element cycle, and standard qi-flow checks to what is visible in one photograph. It reads a frame, not a floor plan — it cannot see your compass heading, your birth chart, the year's flying stars, or the street outside. Treat it as a well-informed second opinion on furniture, light and clutter, not as a substitute for a practitioner who walks the whole home.
